# Ion themes

Themes for [Ion](https://rive.app), one folder each, published to Ion's registry. In Ion, pick Theme… then Browse Themes… to install one.

Ion itself is built in. Each folder is a package: `ion.json` names its theme files, and its LICENSE credits where the colors come from.

- [Ion Mono](ion-mono): White and graphite with international orange as the one color, for values, the caret and anything that asks for attention.
- [Cyanotype](cyanotype): Prussian blue against tea toned paper, with the violet a tannin toner leaves.
- [Sumi](sumi): Ink on washi, with shu vermilion for literal values and ai indigo for the names of types.
- [Halide](halide): The darkroom under its safelight, with no blue for late nights; in light, a golden sepia print.
- [Riso](riso): Two stencil inks, fluorescent pink for values and blue for the rest, with violet where they overprint.
- [Lichen](lichen): Bark slate under three lichens: chartreuse types, blue grey strings and orange values.
- [Bauhaus](bauhaus): The primary triad: blue names types, red marks values, and yellow is the field color.
- [Prism](prism): Refracted light, the syntax colors spread around the wheel at nearly one lightness.
- [Kiln](kiln): Raku smoke black with turquoise crackle and copper luster; celadon over terracotta in light.
- [Tide](tide): Sea glass in deep water: aqua types, amber strings and one warm coral for values.
- [Nomad](nomad): Hydrogen's palette and Ion's first default, with a light palette of its hues darkened to read on paper.
- [Catppuccin](catppuccin): Catppuccin's Mocha, Macchiato and Frappé, each with Latte for light.
- [Rosé Pine](rose-pine): Rosé Pine and Rosé Pine Moon, each with Dawn for light.
- [Tokyo Night](tokyo-night): Tokyo Night and Tokyo Night Storm, each with Day for light.
- [Zed One](zed-one): One Dark and One Light, as Zed ships them.
- [VS Code](vscode): VS Code's default dark and light themes.
- [Shades of Purple](shades-of-purple): Shades of Purple and its Super Dark, with a light palette of its hues darkened to read on paper.
