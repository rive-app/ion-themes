# Shades of Purple

Shades of Purple and its Super Dark, with a light palette of its hues darkened to read on paper.

Themes: Shades of Purple, Shades of Purple Super Dark.

Colors from [Shades of Purple](https://github.com/ahmadawais/shades-of-purple-vscode); the light palette is ours.

In Ion, pick Theme… then Browse Themes… to install it.
